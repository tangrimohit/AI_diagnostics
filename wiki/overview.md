# AKARI Wiki — Overview

System-wide synthesis of the knowledge base: the projector families it covers and the failure domains it documents.

## Scope

- **EB-X49 lamp family**: model page, specs and parts reference.
- **Large-venue laser** PU22 series: platform page and the EB-PU2216B model page.
- **Laser installation**: EB-L260F.
- **Portable**: CO-FH02.

Models on the same engineering platform share a family page; per-model pages link to it.

## Failure domains documented

- Thermal (TH), fan (FN) and thermistor error families.
- Error / warning framework and how the IPS Tool reports them.
- Cooling architecture and model naming conventions.

## Diagnostic tooling knowledge

The IPS Tool is the diagnostic anchor: log capture (TSV), error and warning counters, and the Condition Diagnosis function for laser models.
